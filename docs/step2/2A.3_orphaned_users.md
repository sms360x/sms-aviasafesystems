# 2A.3 — Orphaned users (backfill skipped)

DATE: 2026-10-10
BRANCH: step2-governance

## Finding

The 2A.3 backfill created one membership per known-role user, **except**
users whose non-`SUPER_ADMIN` role has `tenant_id IS NULL` (they cannot be
scoped to a membership tenant) and users with a NULL/unknown role. Two users
matched the first condition:

| uid | email | role | tenant_id | Why skipped |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `2deebb62-51d9-479f-8f3d-1524aacf4de7` | `smd@caanepal.gov.np` | `STATE_SMD` | NULL | Non-`SUPER_ADMIN` known role with no tenant; design says a regulator membership is scoped to the regulator tenant. |
| `dea8c81d-dd65-4b41-8bd9-062f4e0dc14a` | `test-admin@examples.com` | `STATE_SMD` | NULL | Same. |

No users had a NULL or unknown role; these two are the only orphans.

## Why this matters

- The design (2A.1) states cross-tenant regulators (`STATE_SMD`/`CAAN_SMD`)
  carry their **regulator tenant** on the membership. These two have no
  `users.tenant_id`, so the backfill has no tenant to write.
- 2A.4 resolves the request context from the active membership. With no
  membership, these users fall back to the legacy `users.role`/`tenant_id`
  path (compatibility window), so they are **not locked out** — but they are
  not yet represented in the new model.

## Required manual decision

For each user, choose one:

1. **Assign a regulator tenant** to `users.tenant_id` (the tenant they
   regulate for), then re-run the 2A.3 backfill (idempotent; the `NOT EXISTS`
   guard will now insert their membership), **or**
2. Decide they are intentionally tenantless and record how a `STATE_SMD`
   membership with `tenant_id = NULL` should be interpreted at the auth
   boundary (the design only defines `NULL + SUPER_ADMIN` as the platform
   context, per Q5).

This is a finding, not a fix. No code or schema was changed for it in 2A.3.
