### Discrepancies found and resolution

The 2C.4 instruction conflicted with the design of record in three places. All
were resolved **in favour of 2C.1 §12** (the design of record):

1. The instruction grouped the 4 `hazard_*` tables as *outcome-visible* with the
   regulator-oversight branch. **§12 classifies all four as Operator-only** (§6:
   Operator-only = VSRs, CANs, CAPs, internal findings) → **no regulator
   branch**.
2. `psoe_findings` has **no `tenant_id`** → the uuid template cannot apply; the
   policy uses an `EXISTS` subquery against `psoe_assessments` (§5.2 class A,
   realised via the parent).
3. `audit_dispatches.tenant_id` is **text** → the text (slug) template is used
   (`app.current_tenant_slug`), per §5.2 Template B.

`SUPER_ADMIN` (tenants do not write those rows).
