Phase 2B is closed. This document records the intent, the execution item by

---

## 1. Scope of Phase 2B (as planned)

Phase 2B's purpose (Step 2 Plan, workstream W2 — Role model unification) was to
end the three-way inconsistency in the role vocabulary found in Step 1: the
same actor could be named `AIRLINE_ADMIN`/`TENANT_ADMIN`,
`SAFETY_OFFICER`/`OFFICER`, `USER`/`STAFF`, `CAAN_SMD`/`STATE_SMD`, and the
three conflicting role sets (`_RAW_VSR_ROLES`, `CUSTODIAN_ROLES`,
`REPORT_VIEWER_ROLES`) disagreed about who could see raw VSRs and reporter
identity. The design pass also surfaced that the raw-VSR and custodian rules
were *department-based*, which made the department vocabulary (codes vs labels)
a Phase 2B concern as well, so Phase 2B ended up unifying **both** the role model
and the department model.

The plan's items were 2B.1 (design), 2B.2 (canonical role list + schema), 2B.3
(stored-claim migration), 2B.4 (shim/alias removal and set collapse), 2B.5
(closure-path fix), and 2B.6 (tests and documentation). In execution, 2B.4 was
split into **2B.4a** (department model — a prerequisite the design pass
discovered) and **2B.4b** (role cleanup, closure fix, dropdown/backend
validation), and the test/documentation work was folded into 2B.4b and this
close item (2B.5).

## 2. What actually happened

### 2B.1 — Role model design pass

A read-only census of every role declaration, reader, writer, gate, and test
assumption, plus the three conflicting role sets and the closure defect. The SME
answered sixteen decisions (Q1–Q8 recorded in `2B.1_role_model_design.md` §16):
the canonical role list is seven roles; `SAG_MEMBER` is retired into `STAFF`;
regulator sub-roles collapse to `STATE_SMD`; `USER` retires to `STAFF`; `CAMO_*`
/`QA_*` fold into `DEPT_ADMIN`/`STAFF` + department; `rbac.py` is the
module-gate implementation (keep); the raw-VSR/custodian rule is
department-based; and the closure path is fixed to admit `ACCOUNTABLE_EXECUTIVE`
only.

### 2B.2 — Canonical role list + schema

Replaced the ten-member `CANONICAL_ROLES` with the exact seven-role list
(`SUPER_ADMIN`, `STATE_SMD`, `TENANT_ADMIN`, `DEPT_ADMIN`, `OFFICER`,
`ACCOUNTABLE_EXECUTIVE`, `STAFF`) and changed `ROLE_DEFAULT` from `"USER"` to
`"STAFF"`. Added the additive nullable column `tenants.safety_department_name`
(migration `20261012130000`, applied live) with its ORM mirror. Four (plus two)
tests pinning the old `USER`/ten-member values were updated. The
`safety_department_name` UI was deferred to 2B.4a.

### 2B.3 — Stored claim migration (users, memberships, Firebase)

Migrated every stored role value to its canonical form across the three stores:
`supabase/migrations/20261012140000_role_migration.sql` (4 rows in `users`, 4
in `memberships`; idempotent) and `scripts/migrate_role_claims.py` (3 Firebase
claims; idempotent, `--dry-run` supported). The only non-canonical stored value
present was `SAFETY_OFFICER` (→ `OFFICER`). No schema, config, shim, or
auth-boundary change.

### 2B.4a — Department model unification

Defined one canonical department vocabulary in `config.py` — `DEPARTMENT_CODES`,
eleven codes across the six classifications, with labels — and made
`OperationalScope` validate against it at import. Removed
`master_register._DEPARTMENT_ALIASES`; `normalize_department` now returns a
**code**; `DEPARTMENT_SCOPE_PREFIXES` now returns codes. Migrated
`users.department`, `hazards.department`, `cans.department`, `caps.department`
from labels to codes (migration `20261012150000`, applied live) and bound every
department form to a dropdown (including a `GET /api/v1/tenants/departments`
endpoint and the Create/Edit Tenant Safety Department selector). Data migration
only — no schema change.

### 2B.4b — Role model cleanup

Removed the truly dead code (`_ROLE_CLAIM_ALIASES`, the `AuthRole` enum,
`ALLOWED_USER_CREATE_ROLES`). Canonicalized the role lists in place
(`TENANT_ADMIN_ROLES → ["TENANT_ADMIN"]`, `SAFETY_OFFICER_ROLES → ["OFFICER"]`,
`STAFF_ROLES → ["STAFF"]`, `TENANT_WIDE_ROLES` canonical). Fixed the
closure-path defect (`get_accountable_executive` now admits only
`ACCOUNTABLE_EXECUTIVE`). Aligned the Role dropdown to the canonical seven and
added backend 400 rejection of non-canonical roles on the admin create/update
endpoints. Ten same-class test failures were updated to canonical literals. The
residual (frontend legacy strings; the role-set distinction; `CROSS_TENANT_ROLES`
retaining `CAAN_SMD`; `ROLE_DEFAULT_REGISTRATION`; invite/setup-claims
validation) was recorded in `2B.4b_role_model_cleanup.md`.

### 2B.5 — This close document

Records what Phase 2B was intended to do, what it did, the two design decisions,

## 3. The two design decisions

### 3.1 The canonical role list

Seven roles: `SUPER_ADMIN`, `STATE_SMD`, `TENANT_ADMIN`, `DEPT_ADMIN`,
`OFFICER`, `ACCOUNTABLE_EXECUTIVE`, `STAFF`. The base role is `STAFF` (`USER`
retired). Regulator sub-roles (`CAAN_SMD`, `CAAN_INSPECTOR`, `CAAN_ADMIN`,
`CAAN_AUDITOR`) collapse to `STATE_SMD`. `CAMO_*`/`QA_*` fold into `DEPT_ADMIN`
(for `*_ADMIN`) or `STAFF` (for `*_USER`) with the department carrying the
function. `SAG_MEMBER` is retired into `STAFF`.

### 3.2 The canonical department model

Codes, not labels. Defined once in `config.py` from the `OperationalScope` set
(11 codes across the six classifications, `safety` universal), with a single
`DEPARTMENT_LABELS` map and a `department_code()` normalizer. The four
previously disagreeing sources (`OperationalScope.departments`,
`DEPARTMENT_LABELS`, `master_register._DEPARTMENT_ALIASES`,
`department_resolver.js`) were collapsed to this one source. Stored codes in
`users`, `hazards`, `cans`, `caps`.

## 4. The invariant set (what is now true)

- One canonical role vocabulary (seven roles; `CANONICAL_ROLES`).
- One canonical department vocabulary (11 codes; `DEPARTMENT_CODES`).
- `users.role` stores canonical names (2B.3).
- `memberships.role` stores canonical names (2B.3).
- Firebase custom-claim `role` stores canonical names (2B.3).
- `users.department`, `hazards.department`, `cans.department`,
  `caps.department` store department **codes** (2B.4a).
- `normalize_department` returns codes (2B.4a).
- `DEPARTMENT_SCOPE_PREFIXES` returns codes (2B.4a).
- The closure-path defect is fixed — `get_accountable_executive` admits only
  `ACCOUNTABLE_EXECUTIVE` (2B.4b).
- Backend role validation rejects non-canonical roles with 400 (admin user
  create/update; 2B.4b). Department values are not yet validated on write; only
  `join_team` checks against `applicable_departments`. Department validation is a
  residual (§5).
- The Role dropdown offers exactly seven roles (2B.4b).
- Every form captures department as a dropdown, not free text (2B.4a).
- `tenants.safety_department_name` can be set via the UI (2B.4a).

## 5. What is deferred (residuals)

1. The three conflicting role sets (`_RAW_VSR_ROLES`, `CUSTODIAN_ROLES`,
   `REPORT_VIEWER_ROLES`) are **recorded as residuals — not collapsed**. The
   collapse was deferred because the tenant context is not available at their
   call sites. They retain their current members:

       _RAW_VSR_ROLES        {TENANT_ADMIN, SAFETY_OFFICER}
       CUSTODIAN_ROLES       {TENANT_ADMIN, AIRLINE_ADMIN, SUPER_ADMIN}
       REPORT_VIEWER_ROLES   {TENANT_ADMIN, SAFETY_OFFICER, OFFICER, SUPER_ADMIN}

   Collapse requires tenant context at the call sites. → Step 3.
2. `SUPER_ADMIN` remains in `CUSTODIAN_ROLES`. It was never in `_RAW_VSR_ROLES`.
   The proposed removal of `SUPER_ADMIN` from the raw-VSR rule is a residual to
   be applied when the sets are collapsed. → Step 3.
3. Backend department validation is not implemented on write. Only roles are
   validated. → Step 3.
4. `CROSS_TENANT_ROLES` retains the legacy `CAAN_SMD` member; ~40 out-of-scope
   readers still compare the literal `"CAAN_SMD"`. → Step 3.
5. ~28 frontend files contain legacy role strings (`public/`). → Step 3.
6. `can_view_reporter_identity(report, user)` lacks the tenant context needed to
   read `safety_department_name`. → Step 3.
7. `ROLE_DEFAULT_REGISTRATION` uses a legacy default (`"AIRLINE_ADMIN"`, used at
   join-team). → Step 3.
8. Invite / setup-claims role validation against `CANONICAL_ROLES`. → Step 3.
9. Operational-role relabelling in `join.html` (the "Operational Role" dropdown
   is not the authorization role). → Step 3.

## 6. What Phase 2C inherits

- The role model is canonical.
- The department model is canonical.
- The identity model is stable (from 2A): one user = one tenant = one role, read
  from the `users` row.
- The three conflicting role sets from Step 1 are **not** collapsed: 2B.4b
  recorded the distinction and deferred the collapse (`_RAW_VSR_ROLES` /
  `CUSTODIAN_ROLES` uncollapsed; `REPORT_VIEWER_ROLES` unreconciled;
  `CUSTODIAN_ROLES` still contains `SUPER_ADMIN`). The corrected rule
  (`TENANT_ADMIN` or Safety-Department `OFFICER`) is designed but not
  implemented.
- Three RLS-related items remain: the RLS bypass (owner connection +
  Firebase/GoTrue mismatch), the FORCE RLS decision, and the RLS policy rewrite.
- Decision D7 (RLS identity model) and D8 (FORCE RLS vs non-owner role) were
  recorded in the Step 2 plan and need to be revisited now that the app identity
  is stable.

## 8. What Phase 2B achieved, in one paragraph

Phase 2B unified the role and department models. There is now one canonical role
vocabulary (seven roles) and one canonical department vocabulary (11 codes). The
register stores department codes, so segregation operates on stable values. The
closure path admits only the Accountable Executive. Every form that captures a
department is a dropdown. The backend rejects non-canonical roles. The phase was
executed in stages that revealed and corrected several design decisions along
the way (`USER` → `STAFF`, `CAAN_SMD` → `STATE_SMD`, department labels → codes).
