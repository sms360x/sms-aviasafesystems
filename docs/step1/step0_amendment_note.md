# Step 0 Amendment Note — Confidentiality and State Visibility

Date: 2026-10-08 (unsigned)
Amends: Project Boundary Charter Addendum §4 and §8.1.
Amends: Project Step 0 Decision Record G2.

The Boundary Charter's original wording stated that raw operator records never leave the operator. This was too strict and did not reflect ICAO Annex 19 and Doc 9859.

The corrected rule:

1. SDCPS sources.
   VSR — anonymous by design. Contact details held only by the airline's Safety Department; redacted from registers. State sees aggregate intelligence only.
   MOR — named, mandatory. Delivered to the state.
   Audit reports — named. Delivered to the state.
   Accident / Investigation — named. Delivered to the state.

2. Internal registers.
   The state sees the operator's OUTCOME (which hazard, what risk, what barriers, what acceptance). The state does not see the operator's PROCESS (how it was done, by whom, with what internal workflow). The latter is pure administrative work of the airline.

3. Drill-down.
   The state's regulatory right of access to a specific record is exercised by initiating a drill-down, purpose-bound to a specific oversight action, and audited. VSR anonymity is preserved even under drill-down.

4. OIP.
   The Operator Intelligence Pack remains a commercial mechanism for aggregate intelligence. It is not a legal constraint on the state's right to know.

All other Step 0 documents remain in force.
