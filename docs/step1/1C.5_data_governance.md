# Data Governance Record

Status: SME-approved 2026-10-10.
Scope: all data held by the platform across operators and
states.

## 1. Ownership

The platform holds data under one of two ownership rules.
Both state regulators and airlines are tenants in the data
model; the ownership rule distinguishes what each owns.

| Category | Owner | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Operational data | The operator tenant | Hazards, risks, barriers, findings, occurrences (VSR, MOR), corrective actions (CANs, CAPs), verifications, flight diversions, surveys |
| State intelligence | The state regulator tenant | National profiles, N-HRC aggregates, RBO prioritisation, SSP/NASP monitoring, state SPI/SPT |

A state regulator does not own operator operational data.
The state's view of an operator is derived intelligence
(outcome), not the operator's registers (process).
Reference: Boundary Charter Addendum §2–3.

## 2. Retention

Retention rule: **lifetime of the operator's SMS**.

Every data item held by the platform is retained for the
life of the operator's Safety Management System engagement.
There is no fixed retention period. Data is not purged by
age.

Rationale:
    - The SMS record is the operator's evidence base for
      ICAO Annex 19 compliance.
    - Safety data loses value when it is discarded; the
      trend requires history.
    - Under CAAN and ICAO, the safety record is expected to
      persist for the life of the SMS.

On termination:
    - When an operator's tenant is terminated (for example,
      company closure or contract end), the retention of
      their data is a defined offboarding event.
    - Offboarding procedure (export, archival, deletion)
      is a Step 2 or Step 3 process item, not defined here.

Exceptions:
    - Demo data (is_demo = true) is purged during the demo
      lifecycle. Demo tenants hzcons-5542802ee5 and
      trirev-e770db8407 are scheduled for removal.
    - User accounts marked inactive may be retained; the
      retention rule applies to the SMS record, not to
      individual user sessions.

## 3. Access rules per role

Raw data is scoped by tenant and by role. The rules:

| Role | Scope | What they can see |
|---|---|---|
| TENANT_ADMIN (Safety Manager) | Own tenant, full | Full tenant operational data |
| SAFETY_OFFICER | Own tenant, operational | Full tenant operational data, including raw VSR (per 1C.1 + correction) |
| ACCOUNTABLE_EXECUTIVE | Own tenant, aggregate + acceptance | Aggregate views, acceptance authority; no raw VSR |
| DEPT_ADMIN | Own department within tenant | Department-scoped CANs, CAPs, and associated hazards/risks |
| OFFICER | Own department within tenant | Department-scoped CANs, CAPs, and associated hazards/risks |
| STATE_SMD | Cross-tenant (own state's operators) | Outcome-level data (hazard, risk, barrier) and mandatory reports (MOR, accident, audit). No VSR, no internal process |
| SUPER_ADMIN | Platform | Platform administration only; not business data unless explicit |

Cross-tenant access by state users is purpose-bound and
auditable per Boundary Charter Addendum §4–5.

## 4. Confidentiality classification

Data is classified as one of four types, with distinct
handling:

| Classification | Examples | State visibility |
|---|---|---|
| Statutory reports | MOR, accident, serious incident | Delivered to the state by law |
| Voluntary safety reports (VSR) | Voluntary reports | Internal to the operator; aggregate intelligence only at the state |
| Internal registers | Hazard, risk, barrier, findings, CAPs | Outcome visible; process not visible |
| Audit findings | Internal and external audits | Delivered to the state by legal/policy basis |

Reference: Boundary Charter Addendum §4 and the corrected
ICAO confidentiality model (Step 1 plan §0.1, §0.4).

## 5. Data subject to special handling

| Data | Handling |
|---|---|
| VSR narrative | Raw only for Safety Manager and Safety Officer of the owning tenant (per 1C.1 + correction) |
| Reporter identity (VSR) | Raw for the Safety Manager and Safety Officer; reporter contact details stored separately (schema change pending: 1C.1b-ii) |
| Risk acceptance signature | Non-delegable; the AE of the owning tenant (per 1C.2) |
| Hazard closure signature | Currently mis-gated; defect recorded for Step 2 (per 1C.2 residual) |

## 6. Audit trail

- Audit records exist for: user creation/deletion, role
  changes, VSR confidentiality marking, CAAN report reads.
- Audit trail is currently in-memory for escalations
  (documented: 1D.8).
- Durable audit-trail persistence is a Step 2 item.
