Safety intelligence for modern aviation.

SMS360X turns safety data into safety information, and safety information into safety intelligence — so operators, regulators, and states can make defensible decisions.

Data is not information, and information is not yet intelligence.

What SMS360X does

Capture

Occurrences, hazards, risks, and barriers in a single coherent model, aligned with ICAO Annex 19 and Doc 9859.

Connect

Safety data linked across State, Regulator, Operator, and User, with tenant isolation by design.

Decide

Dashboards and intelligence for safety managers, accountable executives, regulators, and state safety programmes.

From frontline reporting to boardroom decisions, SMS360X provides a single source of truth.
